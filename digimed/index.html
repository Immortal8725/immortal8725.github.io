DigiMed Connect

A live South African clinic and research platform: patients find and book care, clinics run paperless intake and scheduling, and research sites manage studies on their own subdomain.

Visit digimed-connect.co.za ↗ (opens in a new tab)Ask me about it

The problem

Clinics still run on paper files and phone bookings. DigiMed Connect connects patients, doctors, clinics and staff on one platform.

Who it serves

  • Patients
  • Doctors
  • Clinics
  • Staff (nurses, receptionists, admins)
  • Research sites
  • Renal care centres
  • Bed facilities
  • Private hospitals
  • Ambulance services

Key features

  • Find & book care

    Search providers by location, service and medical aid, then book a visit with live slot selection.

  • Paperless records

    Consent forms, intake, vitals and history captured digitally instead of in filing cabinets.

  • Multi-tenant clinics

    Each clinic gets its own subdomain with services, staff and direct booking, behind access control.

  • Research workflows

    Research sites recruit volunteers, run eConsent, capture CRFs and track safety on their own subdomain.

  • Role-based dashboards

    Separate experiences for patients, doctors and clinic staff.

  • Privacy by design

    Optional analytics and marketing cookies stay off until users opt in (POPIA / GDPR).

What I built and how

  • RESTful APIs with a clean layered architecture (Controller → Service → Repository) in Spring Boot and PostgreSQL.
  • Patient management, appointment booking and multi-tenant clinic features on the React front end.
  • Real-time features with Node.js and Socket.IO.
  • React
  • Spring Boot
  • PostgreSQL
  • Node.js
  • Socket.IO
